US-004: done. Created runs/ml_contam_detection/approach_cmtv.py implementing Cross-Marker Topology Voting. Loads per-marker Newick trees from protTrees/no_singles/, extracts k=5 nearest genome neighbors per tree, computes Jaccard agreement across markers. Per-marker z-score normalization accounts for inherently noisy markers. Classification: top-per-genome + disagree_frac>=0.6 + combined>=0.80 + (genome_z>=1.0 or marker_z>=1.5). Results: burkholderiaceae50 replacement_only P=0.571 R=1.000 F1=0.727; combined P=0.500 R=0.500 F1=0.500; overall P=0.545 R=0.750 F1=0.632. Chloroflexi poor (R=0.000 replacement_only, R=0.500 combined) — contaminants from close lineages don't disrupt marker tree topology. 23/23 tests pass.

Good recall but more FPs than GCP — XGBoost amplifies marker-level noise on small datasets. Top XGB features: topoknn_score, attachment_gap_marker_z, topoknn_score_marker_z. 23/23 tests pass.
